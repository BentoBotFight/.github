# BentoBot

A note for BentoBot.
![BentoWhite](/assets/fight1.png)


## Motivation
I'm Thomas and I love building robots.
I have 2 friends, Ankitt and Farooq. I wanted to share the robots I built with them.
![Ankitt](/assets/ankitt.png)

However, we're in 3 different countries, Ankitt is in India, Farooq is from Canada, and I'm in Florida.
So I built BentoBot as a way for all 3 of us to control robots and have fun.

This eventually grew into the World's most accessible combat robotics we call it.
I built 3 combat robotics & people from all around the world controlled them in fights with sub-500ms end-to-end latency.
![Robot idea](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcda6b436-d4ef-41ff-82b3-cfcdf6b5a2f7_1094x530.png)


## Live Streams & Fights
We did 17 live-stream fights. 100+ players from Canada, India, Netherland, USA, Columbia, etc.
There was fun to be had, stories to be shared.
![Stream#1](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F26349bcb-3d0f-4cec-8093-bc895cc502f8_2733x1499.png)

We made a Dutch alcoholic gave up on drinking. 
(Now he's addicted to robot fights and occasionally, weed)
We helped a mother of two gain the courage to pursue chemotherapy to live longer.
We made a few churches in India fun again on Sunday.
And for many, this was the most fun they have had with robotics in years.
![Thank you Note](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/w_1456,c_limit,f_webp,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3d50fc19-fa3d-4cb4-8d54-dc9320891fbd_1080x1118.png)

For something built by a guy in a bedroom for 2 weeks, that has been the proudest creation of mine.

## Retirement & Open Source
Due to the logistics and time constraints, I have retired Bentobot. However, I believe it can be useful to the world. 

I'm open-sourcing the whole codebase (website, streaming backend, frontend, robot CAD design) so the next guy/gal can start their own robot fight.
